About the Project

Bespoke residential layout designed to maximize natural light, airflow, and outdoor living while prioritizing energy efficiency and sustainable materials. Every space is carefully composed around family life, blending contemporary architecture with climate-responsive, green building practices.

From concept to construction documentation, the design was developed in close collaboration with the client to ensure the home reflects both their lifestyle and the surrounding landscape.

Design Highlights

Passive Solar Design

Building orientation and openings optimized for natural light and cross-ventilation.

Green Design

Energy-efficient systems and sustainable, locally sourced materials throughout.

Water Conservation

Rainwater harvesting and low-flow fixtures reduce overall water consumption.

The Design Process

Phase 1: Concept & Brief

Understanding the client brief, site conditions, and climate to establish a clear design direction and sustainability goals.

Phase 2: Design Development

Developing spatial layouts, material palettes, and passive design strategies that respond to the site and the client lifestyle.

Phase 3: Documentation & Supervision

Preparing construction drawings and specifications, then supporting the client through procurement and site supervision.
